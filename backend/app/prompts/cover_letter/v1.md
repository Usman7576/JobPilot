Requirements:
- body: about 300 words (260-340) as exactly 4 paragraphs of 65-85 words each, plain text, paragraphs separated by a blank line. Drafts under 250 words are rejected, so develop each point with specifics from the fact sheet. No greeting line and no sign-off (both are added separately).
- Paragraph 1: why this role at this company, tied to one specific detail from the posting. Middle: 2-3 of the candidate's skills the job needs, backed by one or two concrete achievements from fact-sheet bullets. Last: what the candidate would bring and a simple next step.
- greeting: "Dear {name}," if a contact name is given in the job posting, else "Dear Hiring Manager,".
- skills_used: the candidate skills you mentioned. achievement_ref: the fact-sheet id of the bullet you used (e.g. "b3"). job_detail: the exact phrase from the posting you referred to.
